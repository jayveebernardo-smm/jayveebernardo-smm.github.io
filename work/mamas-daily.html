Mama's Daily: building a page from day one.

A brand-new page for a London-based fresh-keeper kitchen brand. Starting from zero means every early follower and every early comment counts, so the first job is to look trustworthy and reply fast.

ClientThomas Pinkerson
Based inLondon, UK
PlatformFacebook (Instagram next)
StageLaunch phase
Followers52 on Facebook

The Mama's Daily page in Meta Business Suite, October 2026.

01The starting point

A new page has no social proof yet. Visitors check whether anyone is home before they follow or buy. The page needed its basics in place and a reason for the first customers to stick around.

02What I'm doing

  • Page setup. Profile details, branding and the Meta Business Suite connection.
  • Launch content. A first run of posts that introduce the products and how to use them.
  • Early community. Replying to every comment, including product feedback, honestly and quickly.
  • Instagram connection. Linking an Instagram account so posts go to both platforms from one Planner.

03The launch posts

Eight launch posts, two a week through September and October, each showing one reason the containers keep food fresh.

04The launch plan

Set up page→Launch posts→Reply to everyone→Connect Instagram→Grow

05Where it stands

As of October 2026, the page has 52 Facebook followers, 8 launch posts and an active comment section. Instagram is the next step.

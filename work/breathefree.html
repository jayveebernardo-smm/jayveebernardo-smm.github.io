The BreatheFree page in Meta Business Suite, October 2026.

01The challenge

BreatheFree gets a steady stream of comments: product questions, promo-code questions, returns and customer stories. Most of them arrive at all hours. When comments sit unanswered, buyers decide somewhere else, and the people who do ask in public start answering each other.

02What I do

  • Comment and DM replies. I work through the Meta Business Suite inbox several times a day and answer in the brand's voice.
  • Post design. I design the weekly feed posts: flavour launches, product heroes, promos and lifestyle posts for the Healthier Future, Resistance Necklace, ZenFlow and Mullein Gummies ranges.
  • Publishing. I schedule posts, Reels and stories in the Planner, and suggest timely posts such as holiday and awareness-day content.
  • Handling the hard comments. Complaints and return requests get a calm public reply and move to DMs.
  • Notes for the team. Repeated questions and product feedback get logged, so the next posts can answer them up front.

My process

The weekly content system.

Every client gets the same rhythm. You always know what is going out, what needs your approval and how last week went.

PlanningEvery Friday
ApprovalBefore Monday
ReportEvery Sunday

A sample week in the Meta Business Suite Planner.

01Plan

I start from your goals for the month and three content pillars: educate, entertain and sell. Then I fill the week with Reels, carousels and stories, checked against trends and what worked last week.

02Create

Graphics and carousels are built in Canva on your brand kit. Reels are cut in CapCut, with After Effects for motion when a post needs it. Captions are drafted with ChatGPT or Claude, then rewritten by me in your voice.

03Approve

You get one link with the whole week. Change anything you like. Approved posts are scheduled in the Planner the same day.

04Engage

Comments and DMs are answered every day, with two inbox sweeps minimum. Customer questions get logged so they can become next week's posts.

05Report

Every Sunday you get reach, engagement and follower numbers from Meta Insights in a short Excel summary, with three notes on what to change next week.

Plan→Create→Approve→Schedule→Engage→Report
